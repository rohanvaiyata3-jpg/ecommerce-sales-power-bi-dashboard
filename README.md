# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

I built this Power BI dashboard to understand how e-commerce data can be turned into useful business insights.

The aim was not only to show total sales, but also to understand customers, products, sales channels, and which states are contributing the most revenue.

## What I created

This dashboard has three pages:

- **Executive Overview** – overall revenue, orders, customers, AOV, monthly trend, sales channels, categories, and states.
- **Customer Intelligence** – customer segments, repeat customer rate, top customers, and RFM analysis.
- **Product & Commercial Intelligence** – product performance, category performance, sales channels, state-wise revenue, and discount vs AOV.

## Some insights from the dashboard

- Total revenue is around ₹35M.
- Online sales contribute more revenue than offline sales.
- Electronics and Home & Kitchen are the strongest categories.
- Maharashtra is one of the highest revenue-generating states.
- Customer segmentation helps identify Champions, Loyal Customers, At Risk customers, and Lost Customers.
